Court card front sprites for Jack, Queen, and King are derived from SVG-cards.

Source: https://github.com/htdebeer/SVG-cards
Original project: SVG-cards by David Bellot, fork maintained by H. T. de Beer.
License: GNU Lesser General Public License v2.1 or later.

Modified for this project by rasterizing the 2x PNG card renders, center-cropping to the local card aspect, and resizing to 500x726 pixels.
